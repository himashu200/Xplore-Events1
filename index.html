What We Offer

3. Faculty Invitation & Coordination

Invitation letters, confirmation tracking, session briefs, travel and hospitality coordination, and continuous faculty communication.

4. Delegate Communication & Helpdesk

Announcement campaigns, reminders, query resolution and a dedicated helpdesk on phone, email and WhatsApp.

5. Sponsorship & Exhibition Management

Prospectus design, sponsor outreach, package customisation, exhibition layout, stall allocation and industry partner servicing.

6. Hotel, Accommodation & Travel Coordination

Room block negotiation, guest rooming lists, check-in support, airport transfers and local transport arrangements.

7. Branding, Printing & Event Collaterals

Event identity, backdrops, standees, banners, badges, certificates, kits, signage and complete print production.

8. WhatsApp, Email & Digital Marketing

Broadcast campaigns, emailers, social media promotion, reminders and targeted outreach to build delegate registrations.

9. AV, Venue & Technical Coordination

Venue liaison, hall layouts, audio-visual setup, slide management, recording, live streaming and technical rehearsals.

10. On-site Registration & Conference Management

Registration desks, kiosk check-in, badge printing, volunteer management, floor coordination and on-ground command.

11. Certificates, Badges & Conference Materials

Delegate and faculty certificates, CME credit documentation, badge design and printing, kits and conference stationery.
